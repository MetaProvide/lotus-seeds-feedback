JOYMA SEEDSLive on staging
  • Payments: refunds, full or partial, from the admin. Payment links for someone who could not pay online, shown next to their order. Every payment has an invoice, served from Stripe and downloadable, plus an email when the payment is received. Courses now go through exactly the same registration and checkout as events. My orders links to each registration and shows the course and its date.
  • Memberships: sliding-scale pricing, a minimum and a suggested amount. The card form appears at signup; nothing gets stuck at "payment pending". My Membership shows the plan's name, the last payment and its invoice, and asks before cancelling. A membership that includes a course opens it in Moodle.
  • Guided setup: offering, tiers and pricing, benefits, terms, review, publish. Nothing is half-saved if you stop midway. Short tutorials in English and French, replayable.
  • Events: warning before you lose unsaved work, a review step before creating, "not ready to publish" names the booking option, a duplicate-title warning, and events already running keep their price.
  • Study spaces: your Moodle courses are now Study spaces. Self-study on its own, included with an event, or private. Live courses with Zoom become ordinary events. One known issue: after paying for a Self-study space, signing in to Moodle can fail; we are fixing it.
  • Cyber Essentials: certified on 20 October 2026, the UK government-backed scheme. A certificate your board can point to; details on the Trust Center.
