What we shipped · 1 of 4

You can now see what a change will do before you make it

The biggest piece of the last three weeks. On 12 August the question was: if I edit a repeating event, what happens to the dates people have already booked? Nobody could answer it by looking at the screen.

What it used to be

You changed a repeating event and Lotus quietly decided which dates to update. Some inherited the change, some did not, and there was no way to tell which was which until afterwards.

What it is now

Editing a series shows you a written summary of exactly which dates will change and which will be left alone, before you commit. Dates you have customised keep their own version. Past events stay frozen.

This is the promise Mahyar made on the last call, and it is live on staging today

What we shipped · 2 of 4

Moodle single sign-in is working, end to end

Last time we showed you the connection. This time it is finished: Jamyang's real Moodle, real courses, and a participant who signs in once.

Browse and register on your own site

Courses appear on the public site alongside events, with their own cards, and someone can register for a course without leaving your pages.

One sign-in, straight through

A student signs in to Lotus and lands in their course. No second password, no second account. Enrolments are driven from Lotus and Moodle stays the authority on who is actually enrolled.

Your courses, and only yours

A centre only ever sees its own course estate. A centre without a Moodle connection no longer shows an empty course list that merely looks broken.

It survives being wrong

Credentials, connection errors and missing courses now say what happened instead of showing nothing and letting you assume it worked.

What we shipped · 4 of 4

Memberships, first iteration

You asked for this repeatedly, and the first version is in. It is deliberately the half that does not involve money, because that half has to be right first.

What is in

Membership plans, configurable benefits, member records, activating and cancelling a membership, excluding a specific event from member access, and the roles to manage all of it.

What comes with payments

Recurring billing, member pricing applied automatically at checkout, and members managing their own membership. Those need the payment rails, and they land with Gate C.

Memberships give access to events. If your centre also expects member discounts, tell us today, because that shapes how the benefit rule is written.

The part that is not features

Legal and security, in earnest

Your boards will ask who holds the data, where it lives, and who has reviewed the security of it. We would rather have real answers ready than assemble them under pressure, so this has its own track and its own people now.

Paul has joined the Lotus team

We have grown the team specifically to put more fuel into legal and security. Paul is driving the data-protection and compliance side with us, rather than it being something we fit around the build.

The documents exist and are being reviewed

The data processing agreement, the service agreement and the participant terms and privacy notice are drafted, with plain versions ready for a centre's own lawyer.

External security review · week of 7 September

An independent reviewer goes over the platform, staging and production, on the same model as a review already delivered for another MetaProvide product.

Then Cyber Essentials certification

Once the review is closed, Lotus goes for Cyber Essentials. That gives your board a recognised certificate to point at rather than our word for it.

We will share the findings with your centres. If your board needs something specific to say yes to Lotus, tell us now, while the scope is still ours to shape.

Before 7 October

Create a real event, and register for it

We are asking again, and we will keep asking, because it is the only thing that tells us whether Lotus works. A month is enough time this round.

  • Build one real event from your actual programme — not a test event. Sessions, formats, pricing, the lot.
  • Register for it yourself, as a participant would, all the way to the end. If your centre has members, try it as one.
  • Change it after you have booked, and watch what the propagation summary tells you it is about to do. That is the piece we most need tested.
