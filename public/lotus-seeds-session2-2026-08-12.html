What we fixed

We found event creation cumbersome, so we simplified it

Watching it back after the kickoff, creating an event asked too much and took too long. Nobody had to tell us. We put it at the top of the list.

What was wrong

Too many steps and too many decisions, with no sense of where you were in the process.

What we changed

The creation wizard is simplified. Descriptions take proper formatting, currencies are searchable, price tiers can be created inline instead of in a separate screen, and errors from earlier steps now actually surface instead of failing quietly.

If it still feels heavy when you try it, tell us today
LOTUS SEEDSLive on staging today
New, and it came from your shapes

Multi session events, and a format choice per session

Your real events made this unavoidable. An event that runs over several sessions, where someone attends week one in the room and week three from home, is normal rather than an edge case, and Lotus could not express it.

1 Per session, not per event

A participant picks their format for each session, so a hybrid course works the way people actually attend it.

2 Capacity that means something

You can cap in person places separately from online ones, and only formats with room left can be picked.

3 It stays on the record

The choice travels with the registration, so you know who is coming to the room and who is joining remotely.

We took the strictest of the three options on the table. Please try to break it.

Also shipped

Courses and one single sign in

Jamyang runs its teaching on Moodle, so this started there. It matters to any centre that keeps course material somewhere other than Lotus.

Your courses, visible in Lotus

Courses sync across automatically and stay in step. You manage enrolments from Lotus, and the course platform stays the source of truth for who is actually enrolled, so the two cannot drift apart.

Sign in once

A student signs in to Lotus and carries straight into their course. No second password, no separate account to remember.

Times that are right for the reader

Lotus now works out the time zone of whoever is looking. If it differs from the event's, both times are shown and the difference is made obvious, instead of one quietly wrong time.

Fewer silent failures

Price tiers show their real names, options no longer pre select themselves on a participant's behalf, and a refused action now says so instead of appearing to work.

Your centre, run by you

Less of Lotus that only we can touch. More of it in your hands, and more of it visible to the people registering.

LOTUS SEEDSCommitted where we're sure, honest where we're not
Why, and what it means for you

What we chose, and what it costs

We found a problem in the events engine that could quietly change someone's booking after they had made it. Building a payment system on top of that would have been the wrong order, so we fixed the foundation and built courses and memberships instead.

Payments are not cut, they are later

Stripe, invoicing and subscriptions all land in the next cycle and are in the final demo. The overall date has not moved.

Lodging has left the MVP

Rooms, beds and meals are out of this build. IVY, this affects you most, and we would rather say it now than in September. It is sequenced after the MVP, and we want to shape it with you when we get there.

If this changes what Lotus is worth to your centre, we need to know that today
