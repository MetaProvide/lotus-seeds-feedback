Before 7 October

Two things to build, and a handful of questions

Building the real thing is the only test that tells us whether Lotus works. Both together are under an hour, and they are the most useful thing you can send us this month.

1Events~30 minutes

Create a real event

Not a test event. One you actually run: sessions, formats, teacher, capacity, your real prices. Then register for it yourself, as a participant would, all the way to the end.

Membership pricing is not in that path yet, so book as an ordinary participant this round. Then change the event after you have booked and read what the propagation summary says it is about to do. That is the piece we most need tested.

2Memberships~20 minutes

Create a real membership

Not a placeholder tier. Build the membership program you would actually sell: your real tier names, your real prices, your real benefits, and which events each tier includes. Publish it.

Nobody can join yet, so nothing you publish charges anyone. But you will find out fast whether your tiers fit, and a published plan has no edit button, so choose as if it were live.

Why the membership one matters most

Payments arrive next, and once a member is charged their terms are frozen. Anything wrong in the shape of your program is cheap to fix this month and expensive to fix after.

And send us one thing that felt wrong

One honest observation from each of you is enough to change the next cycle. The feedback form or the channel, whichever is quicker for you.

Where to try things

The test environment, and who to log in as

Everything lives at stage.joyfuleffort.com. It is a test system: nothing here charges anyone, nothing reaches a real participant, and you cannot break anything we cannot put back. Please do experiment.

Make your own users too

You do not have to test with ours. Register a fresh participant with any email you control, and create additional staff users for your own centre from the admin panel. Testing with an account you made yourself is often the fastest way to spot something that is confusing rather than broken.

Two things worth knowing

Emails from staging are real, so use an address you can open. And please do not change the password on the shared accounts above — other centres are using them the same week you are.

What we need from you

Four questions we cannot answer for you

These are the ones where the right answer differs for each centre, and where changing your mind later is genuinely expensive. Answer in the channel, in the feedback form, or by email. A rough answer today beats a considered one in December.

1

Your tables: what is missing?

Look at the tables in the admin backend — events, registrations, people, memberships. Which fields or filters are missing so you can export the information you actually need?

Be specific: the column your treasurer asks for, the filter you would use every Monday morning, the thing you currently rebuild by hand in a spreadsheet.

2

Changing terms

When you raise a price or change what is included, what should happen to people who already joined? Stay where they are indefinitely, move at their next renewal, or be asked?

And what notice do you feel you owe them?

3

Included, or discounted?

Jamyang's model is inclusive: a member simply pays nothing for an included event. IVY's card model is a percentage off. Today Lotus does the first and not the second.

If your centre needs percentages, saying so now is far cheaper than later.

4

Your back office

We built activate, pause, resume and cancel. We did not build renew, refund, or change someone's billing. Which of those does a real week at your centre actually need?

And one for Jamyang: your Calm Abiding and Special Insight students are already enrolled and paying. When memberships arrive, what should they be offered?
