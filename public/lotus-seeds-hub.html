What to test

1Events

Create—then change—a recurring event

Create a recurring event you actually run. Use real sessions, teacher, capacity and prices. Register for it yourself, all the way through — as an ordinary participant (member pricing will integrate later).

Make changes to the event. Change the teacher, add another session, etc. and read the propagation summary. We need your feedback on whether that summary correctly matches your intention.

2Memberships

Create a membership

Build the membership you would actually sell: use your membership tiers' real names, prices, benefits, and which events each tier includes. When you're ready to lock the membership terms, Publish it.

Then send what you noticed via the feedback form.

4

Your input

Areas where we need your additional input. Each center's situation will differ. A rough reply is plenty. Please reply in the Galaxy channel.

Reporting

Your tables

Look at the tables in the admin backend — events, registrations, people, memberships. Which fields or filters would let you export exactly the information you need?

Be specific: the column your treasurer asks for, the filter you would use every Monday morning, the thing you currently rebuild by hand in a spreadsheet.

Membership

What is built, and what is next

Jamyang, Shantideva, and TNL's membership models are based on access: members can use the materials and register for free for any courses and events included in their plan. Lotus has built this. Please test it.

IVY's model is a percentage discount on lodging and meals. We will build that a little later.

Does your center's membership program involve discounts of any other kind?

Changing membership terms

When you raise a price or change what is included, what should happen to people who already joined? Stay where they are indefinitely, move at their next renewal, or be asked?

How much notice should they get before a change takes effect?

Your back office

Center staff will be able to activate, pause, resume and cancel a membership.

Participants are able to renew, upgrade, and change payment details for their own membership.

We also plan to let center staff issue a refund. Cancel is on the staff list because people often phone the office instead of doing it themselves.

Does center staff need to do anything else for a member? For example: change payment details, upgrade them after a conversation, or extend a membership. If staff currently have to do it, we need to know.

And one for Jamyang: your Calm Abiding and Special Insight students are already enrolled and paying. When memberships arrive, what should they be offered?
